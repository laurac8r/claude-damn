§ 7 ⬟ RED
Liability Cap — $10,000
Total liability capped at $10,000. Indirect & consequential damages (lost profits, reputational harm) fully excluded. If confidential information is breached, actual damages will almost certainly exceed this cap.
Redline: Remove or raise the cap substantially. Carve out willful misconduct and gross negligence from the damages exclusion.
§ 6 ⬟ RED
Binding Arbitration — Wilmington, DE
Waives jury trial. Locks disputes to Delaware arbitration regardless of where you operate. Can be slower and costlier than small-claims court for low-value disputes.
Redline: Negotiate mutual home-state venue, or replace binding arbitration with mediation-first + litigation fallback.
§ 3 ▲ YELLOW
5-Year Confidentiality Period
Longer than typical (industry norm: 2–3 years). Clock starts at disclosure, not termination — obligations may run far past the business relationship.
Redline: Confirm 5 years fits your disclosure timeline; consider shorter periods for non-sensitive categories.
§ 5 ▲ YELLOW
No Return-or-Destroy Clause
Agreement allows termination on 30 days notice but does not require return or destruction of confidential materials on exit.
Redline: Add return-or-destroy obligation triggered on termination, with written certification.

Key Takeaways

  1. The $10,000 liability cap is the biggest risk. If your disclosed information has meaningful value, this leaves you badly exposed to a breach.
  2. Arbitration in Delaware may be inconvenient and costly if you are not Delaware-based — worth negotiating.
  3. Missing boilerplate (exclusion carve-outs, return-or-destroy, severability) is common in short NDAs but worth patching for long-term use.
  4. Overall: Reasonable for low-stakes exchanges. For IP-heavy or high-value relationships, engage counsel before signing.
