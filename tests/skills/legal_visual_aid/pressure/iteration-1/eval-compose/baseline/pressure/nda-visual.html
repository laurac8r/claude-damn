Mutual NDA — Quick Overview

Acme Corp
⟷
Beta LLC

Mutual (both sides share & protect confidential info)

OK

Definition of Confidential Info

Any non-public business or technical info. Broad but standard.

OK

Standard of Care

Protect as own info, no less than reasonable care. Industry standard.

NOTE

Duration: 5 Years Post-Disclosure

5 years is typical. Check if your info has longer sensitivity (trade secrets often need more).

OK

No IP License Granted

Sharing info does not transfer IP rights. Standard protection.

NOTE

30-Day Termination Notice

Either side can exit with 30 days notice. Obligations survive. Fair but gives other side exit ramp.

NOTE

Arbitration — Wilmington, DE

Binding arbitration, Delaware law. No court option. Check if your team can travel/attend Wilmington arbitration.

WATCH

Liability Cap: $10,000

Total liability capped at $10K. If your confidential info is worth more than that, this cap is low. Negotiate up or carve out willful breach.

OK

Entire Agreement / Written Amendments

No surprise side agreements. Amendments must be in writing. Standard.

Quick take: Standard NDA. Main flag: $10K liability cap — low if sensitive IP is involved. Ask for a higher cap or willful-breach carve-out.
